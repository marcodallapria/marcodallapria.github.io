About

I am a PhD candidate in Modeling and Data Science at the University of Turin. My research lies at the interface of Bayesian nonparametrics, stochastic processes, and random combinatorial structures. My doctoral dissertation develops duality-based methods for filtering and inference in two-parameter Poisson–Dirichlet diffusions and related stochastic partition models.

Research Interests

  • Bayesian nonparametric statistics and random partition models
  • Combinatorial stochastic processes and stochastic duality
  • Monte Carlo and sequential inference methods
