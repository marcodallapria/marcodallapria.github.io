Welcome!

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Statistical Sciences, University of Padova (Italy), where I work with Francesco Denti on Bayesian nonparametric methods for complex data (BNP4BNP — Bayesian Non Parametrics for Biclustering and Nested Partitions).

In April 2026 I completed my PhD in Modeling and Data Science at the University of Turin (Italy), under the supervision of Matteo Ruggiero and Dario Spanò.
My doctoral research focused on several aspects of Bayesian inference for random partitions, including duality-based inference for hidden Markov models driven by two-parameter Poisson–Dirichlet diffusions.

Research Interests

  • Bayesian Nonparametrics
  • Stochastic Duality and Filtering
  • Bayesian Consensus Clustering
  • Functionals of Random Discrete Populations
